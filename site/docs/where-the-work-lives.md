# Where does the work live?

*Three questions a Facilitator walks in with. One answer, three times.*

---

When a Facilitator looks at Are-Self for the first time, they usually arrive with three practical questions in some order:

- **Where does my Learner keep their work?**
- **Where do I see what they did — where do they Demo it for me?**
- **Where can my Learners collaborate with one another?**

The honest answer to all three, in Are-Self, is the same word: **the genome**.

---

## What a genome is, in one paragraph

A genome is the persistent shape of a Learner's partnership with their AIs. It carries the identity the Learner created, the pathways they built, the modifiers they grew, the Log of what they explored. It's a single file. It travels. It loads. When the Learner opens Are-Self again next class, the genome is what makes the AIs remember who the Learner is, what they were working on, and what they figured out last time.

Outside Are-Self, you might call this a save file. Inside Are-Self, it is the work itself, because the work in this curriculum is the partnership the Learner grew with the AIs to get to an answer.

---

## How that answers each question

**Where the work lives between sessions.** The Learner's genome lives on the Learner's machine, or on the classroom's machine, depending on the install. Open Are-Self → the genome loads → the partnership picks up where it left off. There is no cloud account standing between the Learner and their work.

**Where the Demo happens.** A Demo in Are-Self is the Learner showing their genome to the Facilitator. The Facilitator loads the genome and sees the whole partnership — the identity, the pathways the Learner built, the Log of Explorations, the questions the Learner asked, the things the Learner figured out. The Demo is the working shape of the relationship the Learner grew.

**Where collaboration happens.** A Learner can hand their genome to another Learner. The other Learner loads it, works alongside it, branches it, contributes to it, or merges their own work back into it. The genome is the medium of collaboration — fellow Learners co-build by exchanging genomes the way a band exchanges stems.

---

## Why one word answers all three

The same artifact carries persistence, Demo, and collaboration because all three are the same thing in Are-Self: showing the partnership the Learner is in. The genome is what shows it.

The Learner's genome stays the Learner's. They Demo it. They share it. They keep it.
